Source distribution
-------------------
The source archive contains wrapper scripts, Dockerfiles, configuration,
documentation, and logo.jpg. No resolved language dependency tree, upstream image,
or model weights are shipped in this archive. The provenance and redistribution
rights of logo.jpg still need maintainer confirmation.

Operator-built software (not published by this repository)
--------------------------------------------------------
Dockerfile: vllm/vllm-openai:v0.25.1 from https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm
adds curl, ca-certificates, flashinfer-python 0.6.13
(https://github.com/flashinfer-ai/flashinfer), and nvidia-cutlass-dsl 4.5.2
(https://github.com/NVIDIA/cutlass). CUDA/NVIDIA components have their own terms.

Dockerfile.open-terminal: ghcr.io/open-webui/open-terminal:0.11.34-slim from
https://github.com/open-webui/open-terminal adds distribution nodejs and npm.

Dockerfile.open-webui: ghcr.io/open-webui/open-webui:v0.10.2 from
https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui adds local user/directory configuration.
Review the exact upstream version's license, branding requirements, bundled
assets, and exceptions; do not assume it is covered by this wrapper's Apache-2.0.

These images contain transitive Python, JavaScript, OS, and native dependencies.
Security workflow SBOMs describe detected installed packages, not every asset.
The built-terminal attribution-candidates.tsv is machine metadata for review,
not legally sufficient attribution. Retain upstream copyright/license/NOTICE
files already in images. Before distributing any derivative image, reconcile
the entire image, collect required license texts/notices and source offers, and
ship reviewed notices both inside the image and with release downloads.

Externally downloaded model assets (not shipped here)
---------------------------------------------------
https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-NVFP4
https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Qwen3.6-27B-NVFP4
https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B

The host download helper retrieves these separately; CI does not download or
execute weights. Operators must review each exact revision's model card, LICENSE,
base-model/quantization terms, tokenizer and other asset notices before use or
redistribution. Revisions are not pinned by the existing downloader; record the
resolved revision and hashes locally. No model licensing determination is made
by the package SBOM or this notice.

External tools/services
-----------------------
Hugging Face CLI/uv and optional OpenCode are operator tools, not shipped runtime
packages. Tavily is an external service subject to its own terms and privacy
policy; outbound searches are not local-only processing.
